Is Reiki safe?

Reiki is gentle and non-invasive, and you remain fully clothed throughout. Sanghamitra, a psychology graduate, guides every step with calm precision. It complements medical care and never replaces it.

Does an online attunement really work?

Your attunement is a live 45-minute video call with Sanghamitra, never a recording. She guides you through every step, and if you feel nothing she repeats it in a second session at no cost.

Will my family call it superstition?

Reiki is non-religious: no astrology, no remedies, no ritual. Begin with a free 15-minute video consultation, or invite a family member to a Family Q&A Call and let them ask Sanghamitra directly.
