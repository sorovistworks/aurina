Three questions almost everyone asks

Is Reiki safe?

Your attunement is a live 45-minute call with Sanghamitra, not a recording, so you hear every step. If you feel nothing, she repeats it in a second call, free.

Will my family call it superstition?

Questions

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Come as you are. There is nothing to prepare and nothing you need to believe beforehand.

Does an online attunement work?

It is a live call, not a recording. You hear every step, and if you feel nothing, Sanghamitra repeats it in a second call, free.

What is the certificate?

It records your Level 1 in the Usui Shiki Ryoho lineage. Reiki is not regulated in India, so this is a lineage certificate, not a government licence.

What if I miss a call?

Each call can be rescheduled once, free, with 2 hours’ notice.

Is Reiki religious?

No. Reiki is non-religious. There is no astrology, no remedies and no ritual.

Can I practise privately?

Yes. Twenty minutes with earphones is enough, and you do not need a special room.

Is Reiki a replacement for medical care?

No. Reiki is a complementary practice. Please keep seeing your doctor and continue any treatment you have been given.

How do I pay?

By UPI or card. Message us to join and we will send you a secure payment link.

How does a distance healing session work?

Healing sessions are coming soon. When they open, we agree a time together, and you rest somewhere quiet at home while Sanghamitra gives Reiki from the studio.

How do I begin?

Message us on WhatsApp to join Level 1, or book a free 15-minute call to talk to Sanghamitra first. For healing sessions, ask us and we will let you know when they open.
