What a session will feel like

  1. Talk

    A short, unhurried conversation about how you are and what brings you in.

  2. Settle

    You lie down, fully clothed, with a blanket and the lights low.

  3. Receive

    Gentle hand positions, resting on or just above the body, in silence.

  4. Return

    Time to come back slowly, sip water and share anything you noticed.

Questions

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Come as you are. There is nothing to prepare and nothing you need to believe beforehand.

Is Reiki a replacement for medical care?

No. Reiki is a complementary practice. Please keep seeing your doctor and continue any treatment you have been given.

How does a distance session work?

Healing sessions are coming soon. When they open, we agree a time together, and you rest somewhere quiet at home while Sanghamitra gives Reiki from the studio.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable. You stay fully clothed throughout.

How do I book?

To join a Level 1 class, send a message on WhatsApp or write to us by email and we will reply with dates. Healing sessions are coming soon; ask us and we will let you know when they open.
