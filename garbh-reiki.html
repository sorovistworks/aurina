Before you buy

Is Reiki safe in pregnancy?

It's gentle touch and breathing, done lying down. Sanghamitra's lineage teaches it at any stage; some teachers prefer to wait until 12 weeks, so tell your gynaecologist you're starting.

Will this help me conceive, or keep my baby healthy?

No, and nobody honest can promise that. This is calm for you and connection with your baby, alongside your doctor's care.

Can the ebook make me Reiki Level 1?

It teaches you everything Level 1 covers, and you can practise from it. Becoming Level 1 means an attunement from a teacher, which Sanghamitra does in her 1:1 course.

I've been told to rest. Can I still do it?

Yes. Everything is done lying down.

I'm still trying to conceive. Is it for me?

Yes. Start now, so the practice is already yours when your baby comes.
